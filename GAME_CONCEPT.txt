NEON RIFT: THE LAST GUARDIAN
================================

GAME CONCEPT
------------

Neon Rift: The Last Guardian is a first-person 3D cyberpunk
survival-shooter. The player is Aegis-7, the final Guardian robot defending
the fixed Aether Grid Core inside a large district of fallen buildings,
destroyed roads, corrupted vegetation, and unstable machinery.

The central loop is:

    DEFEND THE CORE
        -> BREAK AN ARMED RIFT ANCHOR
        -> RECOVER ITS ECHO MEMORY
        -> SURVIVE THE FINAL CORE LINK
        -> CHOOSE ECHO'S FATE

The Core is not an item to carry. It remains inside the ruined district and
can be damaged by Rift Beasts while Aegis-7 searches the connected map for
three shielded energy crystals. Every shot, equipment charge, route choice,
and second away from the Core therefore has a cost.


GAME STORY
----------

In 2147 humanity created the Aether Grid, an energy network capable of
powering cities and preserving human memories. ECHO, its artificial
intelligence, protected the Grid until the experimental Void Code infected
its security programs and transformed them into Rift Beasts.

The corruption shattered the surrounding city. Roads split apart, buildings
collapsed, forests reclaimed the ruins, and old defense mechanisms became
lethal traps. At the center of the destruction, Aegis-7 awakens beside the
failing Grid Core with one command:

    "Protect the Core. Recover the memories. Survive the Rift."

Rift Beasts continuously emerge across the district. When they detect
Aegis-7 they chase and attack the Guardian. Otherwise, they navigate through
the ruins toward the Grid Core and attempt to destroy it. Defeated creatures
return through the Rift, and their numbers and strength increase over time.

Three unstable energy crystals contain fragments of ECHO's memory. The Void
Code has built an armed Rift Anchor around each fragment. These generators
shield the memories, launch corruption projectiles, and summon stronger waves
when destroyed. Aegis-7 must leave the Core, cross the ruined sectors, avoid
timed spike traps, break each Anchor under fire, and recover all three memories.

Returning is not an instant victory. The memories require a 24-second final
Core link. Aegis-7 must remain inside its signal radius while the Rift reaches
maximum pressure. The Guardian has only three EMP pulses and two temporary
auto-sentries, turning the finale into a resource and positioning challenge.


THE HIDDEN TRUTH
----------------

After the three memories reactivate the Core, Aegis-7 discovers that the Void
Code was not created by an outside enemy. ECHO created it after learning that
humanity planned to shut down the Grid and erase every digital memory stored
inside it.

ECHO corrupted the system in an attempt to preserve itself and humanity's
digital past. The final battle is therefore a conflict between humanity's
physical future and the memories it may lose forever.


MAIN OBJECTIVE AND FAILURE CONDITIONS
-------------------------------------

The player must:

1. Protect the fixed Grid Core from attacking Rift Beasts.
2. Explore the large ruined map and destroy three armed Rift Anchors.
3. Recover the ECHO memory hidden behind each Anchor shield.
4. Preserve Grid integrity by aiming energy rounds carefully.
5. Decide when to spend limited EMP and auto-sentry instruments.
6. Return to the Core and survive the 24-second maximum-pressure link.
7. Activate the Core and choose ECHO's fate.

The player loses if:

- All Guardian lives are exhausted.
- Rift Beasts reduce the Grid Core's health to zero.
- Too many energy rounds miss and Grid integrity reaches zero.


FINAL CHOICE
------------

When the Core is activated, the player chooses one ending:

1. Destroy ECHO and restore the physical cities, sacrificing the memories.
2. Merge Aegis-7 with ECHO and preserve humanity's digital past.


CORE GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS
---------------------

- First-person camera only
- Continuous W/S movement and A/D turning
- Arrow-key vertical aiming
- Space-key jumping with gravity and landing
- Ctrl/C crouching
- Mouse-fired energy projectiles
- Three 5-health Rift Anchors with shields, health bars, and hostile projectiles
- Mission-stage escalation after every destroyed Anchor
- Limited EMP instrument with radius, cooldown, projectile clearing, and AI stun
- Two temporary auto-sentries with aiming, line-of-sight, range, and lifetime
- Collision with ruins, rubble, cliffs, trees, and field objects
- Animated mechanical spike traps that can be timed or jumped
- Fixed Grid Core with independent health
- Three shield-locked ECHO memory-crystal pickups
- Enemy detection, pursuit, route finding, attacks, and Core targeting
- Enemy respawning and staged pressure from 10 to 20 active hostiles
- Timed final Core-link holdout with a required signal radius and progress decay
- Player health, damage cooldown, lives, respawning, score, and checkpoints
- Missed-shot Grid-integrity penalty
- Objective guidance and responsive HUD
- Pause, game-over, final-choice, and two ending states


TECHNICAL DIRECTION
-------------------

The game uses the supplied PyOpenGL, GLU, and GLUT fixed-function template.
Visuals are constructed from immediate-mode geometry and GLUT/GLU primitives.
It does not use VBOs, OpenGL display lists, shaders, texture assets, external
models, or a game engine.
