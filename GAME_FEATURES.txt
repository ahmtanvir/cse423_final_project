NEON RIFT: THE LAST GUARDIAN
GAME FEATURES
========================================


1. FIRST-PERSON 3D GAMEPLAY

- First-person cyberpunk survival-shooter experience.
- PyOpenGL, GLU, and GLUT based 3D rendering.
- First-person weapon, hands, crosshair, and camera system.


2. INTERACTIVE RUINED-CITY MAP

- Large map containing destroyed buildings, roads, rubble, trees, cliffs,
  bridges, vegetation, and environmental objects.
- Solid objects block player movement and projectiles.
- Environmental objects provide cover during combat.
- Grid-based map layout controls the world structure, objectives, hazards,
  enemy spawn points, and Core location.


3. CORE DEFENSE MISSION

- Protect the fixed Aether Grid Core from Rift Beast attacks.
- Enemies can attack the Core while the player explores other areas.
- Core health is separate from player health and Grid integrity.


4. THREE RIFT ANCHORS AND MEMORY CRYSTALS

- Three magenta Rift Anchors are placed across the map.
- Each Anchor has 5 health and protects one pink ECHO memory crystal.
- Destroying an Anchor unlocks its memory crystal.
- Destroying an Anchor triggers a new enemy wave and increases mission
  difficulty.
- Collecting a crystal awards 50 points and creates a checkpoint.


5. ENEMY AI

- Standard and Brute Rift Beast enemy types.
- Enemies detect and chase the player when alerted.
- Enemies navigate through the ruins instead of moving through obstacles.
- Enemies can target and damage the Grid Core.
- Enemies have health, attack range, damage, movement speed, and animations.
- Defeated enemies respawn during the mission.
- Enemy health bars appear when enemies are targeted or recently damaged.


6. DYNAMIC DIFFICULTY

- The game starts with 10 active enemies.
- Enemy speed, strength, spawn frequency, and population increase over time.
- Destroying each Anchor adds two enemies to the target population.
- The final Core link increases the target population to 20 hostiles.


7. COMBAT AND PROJECTILES

- Mouse-fired energy projectiles.
- A/D and arrow keys control aiming.
- Player projectiles can hit enemies and Rift Anchors.
- Anchor hits award 15 points and enemy hits award 10 points.
- Destroying an Anchor awards an additional 100 points.
- Missed shots reduce Grid integrity by 4%.
- The mission ends if Grid integrity reaches zero.


8. EMP PULSE SYSTEM

- Q activates the EMP pulse.
- The player starts with 3 EMP charges.
- EMP stuns nearby Rift Beasts for 3.6 seconds.
- EMP removes nearby hostile projectiles.
- EMP has an 8-second recharge and does not damage enemies.


9. AUTO-SENTRY SYSTEM

- E deploys an automatic defensive sentry.
- The player starts with 2 sentry charges.
- Each sentry remains active for 48 seconds.
- Sentries automatically aim at and attack visible enemies.
- Buildings, trees, and other objects block sentry line of sight.


10. MOVEMENT AND PHYSICS

- Smooth forward and backward movement with acceleration and deceleration.
- Turning and vertical aiming use smooth movement calculations.
- Jumping uses vertical velocity and gravity.
- Gravity is set to 980 units per second squared.
- Jump speed is set to 430 units per second.
- Crouching changes the player's eye height and movement speed.
- Player height and vertical velocity are reset when landing.


11. MATHEMATICS AND COLLISION DETECTION

- Euclidean distance calculations determine enemy range, pickup range, and
  equipment effects.
- Sine and cosine calculate player direction, aiming, movement, and animated
  object motion.
- atan2 calculates enemy headings and objective-arrow direction.
- Dot-product calculations determine whether enemies are near the crosshair.
- Circle-radius collision checks detect contact with walls, trees, rubble,
  and other obstacles.
- Movement is divided into small steps to prevent passing through walls.
- Separate X and Y collision checks allow the player to slide along walls.
- Breadth-first-search pathfinding guides enemies around blocked map cells.


12. FIELD HAZARDS

- Timed mechanical spike traps with nine spikes.
- Amber lights indicate a preparing or retracted trap.
- Red lights and raised spikes indicate danger.
- Players can avoid traps by timing movement, moving around them, or jumping.


13. PLAYER HEALTH AND PROGRESSION

- Player starts with 100 HP and 3 lives.
- Enemy and trap damage reduce player health.
- Damage cooldown prevents repeated damage in a single instant.
- Respawning restores full health at the latest crystal checkpoint.
- Score increases through combat, crystal collection, Core activation, and
  the final decision.


14. FINAL CORE-LINK CHALLENGE

- Collecting all 3 memories unlocks the final Core link.
- The player must remain inside the green signal ring for 24 seconds.
- Link progress decreases when the player leaves the ring.
- Enemy pressure reaches its maximum during the final defense.


15. MULTIPLE ENDINGS

- Press 1 to destroy ECHO and restore the physical cities.
- Press 2 to merge with ECHO and preserve humanity's digital memories.
- Both choices complete the game and award 500 points.


16. VISUAL EFFECTS AND GAME STATES

- Neon cyan, magenta, red, and green visual style.
- Animated enemies, Anchors, crystals, traps, and projectiles.
- Particle bursts, floating score text, muzzle flashes, hit markers, and
  damage effects.
- Weapon recoil, weapon bob, camera shake, and stun effects.
- HUD with health bars, progress bars, objective guidance, and status values.
- Playing, paused, game-over, victory, restart, and ending-choice states.
