NEON RIFT: THE LAST GUARDIAN
PLAYER MANUAL
========================================


1. GAME OVERVIEW
----------------

You play as Aegis-7, the final Guardian robot protecting the Aether Grid.
The Grid powers humanity's remaining cities and stores millions of digital
memories. Its artificial intelligence, ECHO, has been corrupted by the Void
Code, turning its security programs into hostile Rift Beasts.

The Grid Core is failing inside a ruined city filled with collapsed
buildings, destroyed roads, corrupted forests, and mechanical traps. Three
pink energy crystals scattered across the map contain missing pieces of
ECHO's memory. Each memory is imprisoned inside an armed Rift Anchor, and
the Core can only awaken after a final defended data link.

The Core is a fixed structure. It is not an item that the player carries.
Enemies can attack it whenever Aegis-7 is away, so exploration and defense
must be balanced carefully.


2. MAIN OBJECTIVE
-----------------

To complete the game:

1. Begin beside the glowing Aether Grid Core.
2. Defend yourself and the Core from approaching Rift Beasts.
3. Follow the objective arrow to locate a magenta Rift Anchor.
4. Dodge the Anchor's corruption shots and shoot its exposed heart 5 times.
5. Collect the pink memory only after its shield disappears.
6. Repeat this in all 3 ruined-city sectors while enemy pressure increases.
7. Avoid mechanical spike traps or jump over them at the correct time.
8. Follow the objective arrow back to the Grid Core.
9. Enter the green signal radius to begin the 24-second final Core link.
10. Stay in the radius and defend the Core against the maximum enemy wave.
11. When the link reaches 100%, choose ECHO's fate by pressing 1 or 2.


3. CONTROLS
-----------

W                 Move forward
S                 Move backward
A                 Turn/aim left
D                 Turn/aim right
Left Arrow        Turn/aim left
Right Arrow       Turn/aim right
Up Arrow          Aim upward
Down Arrow        Aim downward
Space             Jump
Ctrl or C         Crouch/stand
Left Mouse Click  Fire an energy projectile
Q                 Discharge a limited EMP pulse
E                 Deploy a limited auto-sentry
P                 Pause/resume the game
R                 Restart after winning or losing
1                 Choose to destroy ECHO at the ending
2                 Choose to merge with ECHO at the ending
Esc               Exit the game

The game uses a first-person camera only. Movement accelerates and slows
smoothly while keys are held or released.


4. UNDERSTANDING THE HUD
------------------------

HP
    Aegis-7's current health. Enemy attacks and active traps reduce HP.

LIVES
    The number of Guardian lives remaining. Losing all lives ends the game.

SCORE
    Increases by defeating enemies, collecting memories, activating the Core,
    and completing the final decision.

CORE
    The Grid Core's remaining health percentage. Rift Beasts can damage it.
    If it reaches 0%, the game ends immediately.

GRID
    Aether Grid integrity. Every projectile that expires or strikes an
    obstacle without hitting an enemy reduces Grid integrity by 4%.

MISSED
    Counts wasted energy projectiles.

ANCHORS 0/3 // MEMORIES 0/3
    Shows how many memory shields have been destroyed and memories recovered.

EMP [Q]
    Shows remaining EMP charges and its recharge time after use.

SENTRY [E]
    Shows how many temporary auto-sentry instruments remain.

HOSTILES
    Shows the number of active Rift Beasts in the district.

FINAL CORE LINK
    Appears after the return to the Core and shows upload completion. The
    green bar and large ground ring mark the required defense phase.

OBJECTIVE ARROW
    Points toward the nearest uncollected crystal. After collecting all three,
    it points back toward the Grid Core.

TIME
    Shows survival time. Enemy pressure increases as time passes.


5. RIFT ANCHORS AND PINK MEMORIES
---------------------------------

The floating pink objects are ECHO memory crystals. There are 3 in total,
but they cannot simply be collected. Each one is shielded by a tall magenta
Rift Anchor with 5 health. A locked memory floats brightly above its Anchor
inside three purple rings; after the Anchor breaks, it drops into the pink
ground beam where Aegis-7 can collect it.

At every memory site:

1. Use cover while the Anchor fires corruption projectiles at Aegis-7.
2. Aim at the Anchor's glowing heart and hit it 5 times.
3. Destroying it triggers a new enemy wave and increases later enemy speed.
4. Walk into the now-bright pink memory to recover it.

Anchor shots cause 14 damage. Their fire becomes faster as the mission
progresses. A shot that hits an Anchor counts as a valid shot and does not
damage Grid integrity.

Collecting a crystal:

- Adds one recovered ECHO memory.
- Adds 50 points to the score.
- Updates the respawn checkpoint.
- Reveals the next objective through the HUD arrow.

After the third crystal is collected, the HUD displays:

    ENERGY READY // START CORE LINK

Return to the large glowing Core near the starting area and walk close to it.
This starts the final defense; it does not immediately finish the mission.


6. THE GRID CORE
----------------

The Core is the main object that Aegis-7 must protect. It has independent
health and changes appearance when severely damaged.

Rift Beasts behave in two ways:

- When they detect or are attacked by Aegis-7, they chase the player.
- Otherwise, they navigate through the ruins and attack the Grid Core.

Do not remain away from the Core for too long. Eliminate nearby enemies before
leaving to search another district.

FINAL CORE LINK

After all memories are recovered, enter the Core to start a 24-second upload.
A large green ring marks the signal radius. Upload progress rises only while
Aegis-7 remains inside that ring and slowly falls while outside it. The active
enemy target rises to 20, enemies move faster, and replacements arrive more
quickly. The ending choice appears only after the link reaches 100%.


7. COMBAT AND PROJECTILES
-------------------------

Aim with A/D or the arrow keys and fire with the left mouse button. The center
crosshair shows the projectile direction.

Combat rewards:

- Each successful enemy hit adds 10 score points.
- Each Anchor hit adds 15 points, and destroying one adds 100 more.
- Strong brute enemies require more hits.

Important: missed shots damage Grid integrity. A projectile counts as missed
when it expires or hits a wall, building, rubble, or tree without striking an
enemy. Fire carefully instead of repeatedly shooting without a target.


8. ENEMIES
----------

Standard Rift Beast
    Fast corrupted creature that chases Aegis-7 or attacks the Core. It can
    rotate through a full 360 degrees to face its target and alternates
    animated left- and right-hand claw strikes when attacking.

Brute Rift Beast
    Larger and stronger variant with more health and greater damage.

The game begins with 10 active enemies. Defeated enemies return through the
Rift, more enemies become active over time, and destroying each Anchor adds
two more enemies to the target population. The final Core link raises the
target to 20 hostiles and gives them an additional speed boost.


9. GUARDIAN INSTRUMENTS
-----------------------

EMP Pulse — Q
    Aegis-7 carries 3 EMP charges. A pulse stuns every nearby Rift Beast for
    3.6 seconds and destroys hostile Anchor projectiles inside its radius.
    It has an 8-second recharge between uses. The EMP does not damage enemies,
    so use the breathing room to shoot, escape, or protect the Core.

Auto-Sentry — E
    Aegis-7 carries 2 deployable sentries. Each remains active for 48 seconds,
    rotates toward visible enemies, and automatically fires within range.
    Walls, ruins, and trees block its line of sight. Placement matters: save a
    sentry for a dangerous Anchor site or the final Core-link defense.

Both instruments have limited mission-wide charges; they are not refilled
after use or after respawning.


10. FIELD TRAPS
---------------

Dark metal pressure plates containing nine spikes are placed in several
areas. Their corner lamps indicate the current cycle:

- Amber lamps: the trap is preparing or retracted.
- Red lamps and raised spikes: the trap is dangerous.

Cross while the spikes are retracted, move around the plate, or press Space
to jump over it. Raised spikes cause heavy damage when the player's feet are
close to the ground. A correctly timed jump avoids the damage.


11. HEALTH, LIVES, AND RESPAWNING
---------------------------------

Aegis-7 starts with:

- 100 HP
- 3 lives

When HP reaches zero, one life is lost. If lives remain, Aegis-7 respawns with
full health at the latest crystal checkpoint. If no lives remain, the mission
ends in game over.

Damage has a short cooldown, preventing every enemy or trap from removing all
health in one frame.


12. WIN AND LOSS CONDITIONS
----------------------------

YOU WIN THE MISSION BY:

- Destroying all 3 armed Rift Anchors.
- Collecting all 3 ECHO memory crystals.
- Returning to the Grid Core.
- Surviving inside the signal radius until the final link reaches 100%.
- Selecting one of the two final decisions.

YOU LOSE THE MISSION IF:

- All 3 Guardian lives are exhausted.
- Rift Beasts reduce Core health to 0%.
- Missed projectiles reduce Grid integrity to 0%.


13. FINAL DECISION
------------------

After activating the Core, ECHO reveals that it created the Void Code to stop
humanity from shutting down the Grid and erasing all stored memories.

Press 1: DESTROY ECHO
    Restore power to the physical cities, but sacrifice the stored memories.

Press 2: MERGE WITH ECHO
    Preserve humanity's digital memories by merging Aegis-7 with ECHO.

Neither decision is presented as completely correct. The motive of the ending
is to make the player choose between humanity's physical future and its
digital past.


14. SURVIVAL TIPS
-----------------

- Check CORE and GRID percentages frequently.
- Fire only when an enemy is near the crosshair.
- Clear enemies near the Core before exploring.
- Follow the objective arrow rather than wandering without direction.
- Strafe behind ruins between Anchor shots, then fire at the glowing heart.
- Use destroyed buildings and rocks to control enemy movement.
- Watch trap lights before stepping onto a pressure plate.
- Jump over an active trap if there is no safe route around it.
- Collecting a crystal creates a new checkpoint.
- Save at least one EMP or sentry for the 24-second final Core link.
- Place sentries where buildings and trees do not block their line of sight.
- Stay inside the green ring during the final link or progress will decay.
- Brute enemies are larger and should be prioritized near the Core.
